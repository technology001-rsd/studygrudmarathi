4.5 Joan of Arc

George Bernard Shaw

objective
✅ Answer:
Play: Joan of Arc (Saint Joan) Setting: Period — 1429 (during the Hundred Years War between England and France) Location: Captain Robert de Baudricourt's castle in France Scene of the given play: The steward reports to Robert about a girl (Joan) who wants to meet him; Joan enters and requests horse, armour and soldiers to go to the Dauphin; Robert hesitates but eventually agrees. Characters: Robert de Baudricourt, Steward, Joan of Arc, Bertrand de Poulengey (Polly) Main Plot: Joan, a 17-year-old peasant girl, convinces Captain Robert to give her soldiers and armour so she can go to Chinon to meet the Dauphin and then free Orleans from English control.

✅ Answer:
Her confidence: 1. "You are to give me a horse and armour and some soldiers, and send me to the Dauphin." 2. "I have arranged it all. You have only to give the order." Her courage: 1. "I will teach them all to fight for France." 2. "You and Polly will live to see the day when there will not be a single English soldier on the soil of France."

subjective
✅ Answer:
(a) Joan wanted to meet the Captain squire to ask for a horse, armour and soldiers to go to the Dauphin and then free Orleans from English control. (b) Joan did not ask for many soldiers because she had faith that with God on her side, even a few brave men would be enough. She said "Three men will be enough for you to send with me." (c) They offered to accompany Joan because there was "something about her" that inspired them. Poulengey said, "Her words have put fire into me. I think the girl herself is a bit of a miracle." (d) French soldiers were always beaten because they were "fighting only to save their skins and the shortest way to save your skin is to run away." They lacked motivation and courage. (e) Robert said "I wash my hands off it" meaning he would take no further responsibility for the decision. He was reluctant but agreed, letting Joan go despite his doubts.

activity
✅ Answer:
Indian women who led battles: Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, Rani Karnavati of Mewad, Razia Sultana, Kittur Chennamma, Rani Durgavati. Similarities between Rani Lakshmibai and Joan of Arc: 1. Both fought bravely against foreign powers (British for Rani Lakshmibai; English for Joan of Arc). 2. Both led armies despite being women in male-dominated eras. 3. Both became symbols of courage and inspiration for future generations. Contrasts: 1. Rani Lakshmibai was a queen; Joan of Arc was a peasant girl. 2. Rani Lakshmibai died in battle; Joan of Arc was captured and executed. 3. Rani Lakshmibai fought for her kingdom; Joan of Arc fought for her country driven by divine visions.
