✅ Answer:
Title: Aditi's Green Gift Once there was a young girl named Aditi who loved nature. She noticed that many trees in her neighbourhood were being cut down for construction. The air became polluted and the birds stopped coming. One summer, the heat became unbearable. Aditi's grandmother told her that when she was young, the area was full of shady trees. Aditi felt sad and decided to do something about it. She collected seeds from the park, prepared small pots, and started growing saplings. Her friends joined her. They planted saplings along the roadside and near the school. Years passed, and the saplings grew into tall trees. Birds returned, the air became cooler, and people enjoyed the shade. Everyone praised Aditi for her efforts. ... and so, Aditi decided to plant more trees. Moral: Small actions can lead to big changes.
