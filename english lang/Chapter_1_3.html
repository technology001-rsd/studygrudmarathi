subjective
✅ Answer:
Title: Basketful of Moonlight Poet: Sunil Sharma (translated from Dogri) Rhyme scheme: Free verse (no fixed rhyme scheme) Favourite line: "I want to light the dark route / so that my father returns early." The poem expresses a child's innocent wish to borrow moonlight from the moon and sow it along the dark village path. The central idea is love for the father and concern for the safety of villagers who walk home in darkness. The child's imagination turns moonlight into seeds that can be planted like trees. Figures of speech: Repetition ("Omoon, give me moonlight"), Alliteration ("small, small moons"), and Metaphor (moonlight as something that can be sown). The language is simple, lyrical and full of childlike wonder. I like this poem because it shows a child's pure heart and creative imagination, and reminds us to care for the people we love.
