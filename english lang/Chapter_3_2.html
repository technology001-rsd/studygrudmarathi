subjective
✅ Answer:
Meena was a pessimistic LIC officer who always complained about everything. She had no positive words for anyone and made people around her feel depressed. The writer, Sudha Murthy, was her friend. Years later, they met unexpectedly at Flora Fountain in Mumbai during heavy rain. To the writer's surprise, Meena was now cheerful and happy. Curious about this change, the writer asked Meena what had transformed her. Meena revealed that a beggar who used to sit outside her house with his granddaughter had changed her life. One rainy day, she saw them playing joyfully in the rain despite their poverty. They were happy with nothing. This scene made Meena realize how ungrateful she was. She decided to change her attitude using the beggar as her role model. It took Meena two years to become a positive person. She now finds happiness in small things. Although the beggar died, she showed gratitude by sponsoring his granddaughter's education.
subjective
✅ Answer:
I worked in Bangalore in LIC. Sudha was my friend. I was always unhappy and pessimistic. I never had anything positive to say about anyone. One day, I was transferred to Bombay. Years passed. One rainy day, Sudha met me at Flora Fountain. She was surprised to see me so cheerful. She asked me what had changed me. I told her about a beggar who used to sit outside my house with his granddaughter. I used to give them leftovers. One monsoon day, I saw them playing happily in the rain. They had nothing — no food, no money — yet they were overjoyed. I realized I had so many comforts but was never happy. I felt ashamed. That day, I decided to change my attitude using the beggar as my role model. It took two years, but now I am always happy. I find joy in everything.
