✅ Answer:
(a) Theme: A person begins to "die slowly" when they live passively—avoiding travel, reading, change, passion and risk. To truly live we must be ourselves, keep our self-esteem, break routines and follow our dreams. (b) Evidence: "If you do not go after a dream, / If you do not allow yourself, / At least once in your lifetime, / To run away from sensible advice…" (c) Three dulling habits: becoming a slave of your habits; walking every day on the same paths; not changing your routine; not wearing different colours; refusing to speak to those you don't know (any three).

subjective
✅ Answer:
The poem is written in free verse with no fixed rhyme scheme and uses repetition (anaphora) of "If you do not…" and the refrain "You start dying slowly…". It matches 1.3 "Basketful of Moonlight" (free verse, no rhyme). It differs from 1.1 "A Teenager's Prayer", which has a regular stanza form and an abcb rhyme scheme.
subjective
✅ Answer:
The expression "You start dying slowly…" is repeated (four times, once after each block). The poet emphasizes it to show that every missed chance to live fully—travel, read, love, change, take risks—causes a slow death of the spirit, making the warning unforgettable.
