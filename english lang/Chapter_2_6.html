✅ Answer:
1959 – UNESCO launched an international campaign to save the Abu Simbel Temples in Egypt. 1965 – A White House Conference in the USA called for a "World Heritage Trust". 1968 – IUCN presented similar goals at the UN conference in Stockholm. 1972 – The Convention concerning the Protection of World Cultural and Natural Heritage was adopted by UNESCO on 16 November. 2009 – There were 890 World Heritage Sites in 148 countries.
