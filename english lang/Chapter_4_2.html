✅ Answer:
The proverb means that if we have strong determination and a firm will to achieve something, we will surely find a way to do it, no matter how difficult the circumstances. Difficulties and obstacles are part of life, but a determined person never gives up. They keep trying until they succeed. Bholi is a perfect example. She had a stammer and was considered a simpleton. But with the teacher's encouragement and her own willpower, she overcame her stammer and became a confident speaker. By the end, she had the courage to refuse a greedy bridegroom and stand up for herself. Similarly, Marie Curie, despite extreme poverty, pursued her studies and discovered radium. Great people like Stephen Hawking, Helen Keller and Mahatma Gandhi achieved what seemed impossible because of their strong will. The key is to believe in ourselves, stay focused, and never stop trying. As they say, "Where there is a will, there is always a way." Every problem has a solution — we just need the will to find it.
