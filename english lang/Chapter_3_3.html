3.3 Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening

Robert Frost

objective
✅ Answer:
(a) "He will not see me stopping here / To watch his woods fill up with snow." (b) "He gives his harness bells a shake / To ask if there is some mistake." (c) "My little horse must think it queer / To stop without a farmhouse near." (d) "The woods are lovely, dark and deep, / But I have promises to keep, / And miles to go before I sleep." (e) "His house is in the village, though" — suggesting a traditional village setting. Also, the use of a horse-drawn carriage indicates a time before modern automobiles.
