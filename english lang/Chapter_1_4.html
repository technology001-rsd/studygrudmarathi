✅ Answer:
Sample SMART plan: (a) Specific: 1. English – 4 units, 2. Maths – 5 units, 3. Science – 6 units, 4. History – 4 units, 5. Geography – 3 units, 6. Marathi – 4 units. (b) Measurable: Test I – Poor, Test II – Fair, Semester I – Good (showing improvement). (c) Achievable: Target 80% and above in SSC exams. (d) Realistic: Make a study timetable and follow it; revise daily; take help when needed. (e) Time-bound: English – 5 days, Maths – 7 days, Science – 8 days, History – 5 days, Geography – 4 days, Marathi – 5 days.

✅ Answer:
Gerund examples: setting (goals), quantifying, reading, writing, executing, finding, tackling, creating. Infinitives: to drink, to read, to be, to execute, to illuminate. Finite verbs change with subject: "Preeti was scornful" (past, singular); "They are sportsmen" (present, plural). Word class (sample): Noun – tackle, absence, imagination, expert; Adjective – aggressive, natural, important, specific; Adverb – directly, exactly, clearly, precisely; Verb – execute, achieve, create, trumpet; Pronoun – we, it; Preposition – of, from, but; Conjunction – and, if; Interjection – oh. Changing word class: verb→noun: doubt ("I doubt" → "a doubt"); laugh, walk, catch, throw, turn. noun→verb: peel ("potato peels" → "peel the potato"); nurse ("a nurse" → "nurse a patient").
